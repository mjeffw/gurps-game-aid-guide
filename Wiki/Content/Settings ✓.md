## Recommended Setting Values

The following is minimal list of settings everyone who wants GURPS tactical combat, and easier operation.

### Actor Settings

* ✅ **[Calculate Encumbrance](Settings%20Subpages/Actor%20Settings.md#calculate-encumbrance)**: Checked
* ✅ **[Convert x2/x6 Range to Yards](Settings%20Subpages/Actor%20Settings.md#convert-x2-x5-range-to-yards)**: Checked
