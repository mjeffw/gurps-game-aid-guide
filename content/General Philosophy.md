The origins of GURPS Game Aid and how it works has its roots in the general philosophy Nose developed from the beginning.
## Character Creation
First, character creation was never an original goal of GGA. Nose knew that [GURPS Character Assistant](https://www.sjgames.com/gurps/characterassistant/) (GCA), the official GURPS character creation software, and [GURPS Character Sheet](https://gurpscharactersheet.com) (GCS), has already tackled this and had several decades worth of development behind them. 

Instead of replicating that functionality with all of the inherent complexity, GGA allows the player to import a GCA or GCS character directly. This allowed the team to release GGA much earlier than would otherwise be possible, and mostly with full support of whatever GCS and GCA had already implemented. (Missing is some of the less-than-standard features such as custom attributes.)

Keeping the character creation rules out of GGA creates some serious limitations: for example, GGA does not know how every Trait, Skill, Spell, piece of Equipment, etc., affects the character's ST and therefore cannot easily apply a ST buff to increase muscle-powered melee damage.

Most RPG groups handle this by keeping the "canonical" copy of the character in GCS or GCA, and doing a quick edit and reimport when important things change on the character. There's very little friction in doing so -- you can reimport a character in less than a second or two with the right settings.

_In the upcoming v1.0.0 release, we are reversing that decision and developing much of the GCS functionality, with the author's permission, directly into GGA._
## Content
GGA strives to not copy any text from any SJGames copyrighted material into the system.

Our agreement with SJGames is that we will not copy any protected text into the system itself, nor will we automate the system such that player and GM choice and agency are removed. 

The exact limits of that automation are quite fuzzy; we do a lot of tiptoeing around them and will err on the side of less automation if we think we are leaning too hard into playing the game for the player. (That is in fact one of SJGames's concerns: were we creating a virtual tabletop or a video game that played GURPS?)

The limitation on copyrighted material is why the text of Traits, Skills, Spells, and tables, etc.,  are not included in the system. We generally provide a way for the user to add some of this information (especially around tables). 

We also endeavor to supply a [[PDF Link ✓]] from the item to the rule book in which it appears. If you own the PDF and have uploaded it into Foundry, it will open automatically. Otherwise it will send you to SJGames's [Warehouse 23](https://warehouse23.com) or other internet storefront where you can purchase the PDF.
### A Note About Versioning
_GGA has been around for 6 years with 225 releases. Why is it still versioned as 0.18.20?_

Nose has always maintained that not having a v1.0 release was our way of communicating that we aren't "official" -- meaning SJGames isn't just tolerating our system, but actively supporting or recommending it. 
## Customization
The GGA motto has always been "Your GURPS. Your way." We try to live up to that standard. Many of the features can be opted into or out of, and we try to offer sensible defaults and options to the users, as you will see as you go through the rest of this User's Guide.
